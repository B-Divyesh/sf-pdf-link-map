Policy note · 28 August 2026

Privacy

PDF Link Map is designed to keep document inspection on your machine. The CLI reads the PDF and optional manifest you name, writes the report you request, and sends nothing to us. It contains no telemetry and never requests external links found in a PDF.

Website data

The public site does not use analytics, advertising cookies, or third-party scripts. Its specimen data is bundled with the page. A service worker may cache public site files on your device so documentation remains available offline.

License data

If you buy or restore the optional Team unlock, your browser stores the license token under sb_license:pdf-link-map and a timestamped verification result in local storage. The token is sent only to Sociobot’s license verification endpoint, at most once per day after the first check. You can remove it by clearing this site’s storage.

Checkout and refunds are handled by Sociobot/Dodo as merchant of record under their own policies. PDF files and generated reports are never part of checkout or verification.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the project’s source repository.
