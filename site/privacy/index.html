Policy note · 30 August 2026

Privacy

PDF Link Map keeps document inspection on your machine. The CLI reads the PDF and optional manifest you name, writes the report you request, and sends nothing to us. It contains no telemetry and never requests external links found in a PDF.

Website data

The public site does not use analytics, advertising cookies, third-party scripts, accounts, checkout, or license storage. Its specimen data is bundled with the page. A service worker may cache public site files on your device so documentation remains available offline.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the project’s source repository.
